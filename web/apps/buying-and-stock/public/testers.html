Buying and Stock: the testers' brief

One page about what this slice is, what it does, and what it does not do yet. Everything here is plain speech: if the box and this page disagree, the box is wrong and that is a bug worth reporting.

What this is

This is the procurement manager's slice, signed in as Procurement Manager, on the Buying and Stock board. The desk reads the open purchase orders, the incoming list, and the reorder worklist, and drafts, places and tracks a purchase order. Receiving against an order is later (the yard's Yard Pick and Receive lands the goods; the procurement manager's desk is the screen that drafts and tracks the order, not the screen that closes the gap on the yard's count). Vendor bills are later (Payables and Receivables posts them).

How to sign in

On the front door, tap Procurement manager and the Buying and Stock board opens under that role's name. The card's line is "Buy the branch's stock and track what is incoming." The tile the card opens is Buying and Stock; the desk inside the tile is the procurement manager's, with the tabs Purchase orders, Receiving (later), Incoming, Vendors (later) and Vendor bills (later). On this lane the role is live: the card is tappable, the sign-in rides the front door's handoff, and no second sign-in is asked for.

The Buying and Stock runs one role on this lane, the Procurement manager (this one). The other roles have their own cards on the same front door; a card a tester can tap opens its own app, and a card marked Coming cannot be tapped yet.

The demo world

The desk carries the same invented dealer as the rest of this box (Hardscape House, demo), with two branches, Trenton and Kingston. Every product code begins with DEMO- and every vendor name carries (demo). The procurement world is the dibbits seed's product catalogue and the procurement lane's own seeded suppliers and orders.

  • Three demo suppliers: VND-01 Granular supplier (demo), thirty-day terms, an ordering desk email on file; VND-02 Paver supplier (demo), thirty-day terms, an ordering desk email on file; VND-03 Edging and tools supplier (demo), fifteen-day terms, no ordering desk email on file yet. The order book's other thirty real brands (warranty migration, dibbits seed) are not the demo vendor set; the tester's edits stay on the demo set.
  • Two open purchase orders: DEMO-TRT-P01 at Trenton, vendor VND-02, ORDERED, expected in three days, two lines (P02 six hundred and P03 two hundred forty, the P02 and P03 codes the dibbits seed plants); DEMO-KGN-P02 at Kingston, vendor VND-01, ORDERED, expected in five days, two lines (P08 forty and P10 one hundred).
  • Ten products the seed carries by code: P01 DEMO-PAV-HOLLAND Holland Paver Charcoal, P02 DEMO-PAV-BRUSSELS Brussels Dimensional Sandstone, P03 DEMO-PAV-COURTSTONE Courtstone Basalt, P04 DEMO-WALL-BLOCK Retaining Wall Block 8x18, P05 DEMO-STEP-SLAB Step Slab 24x48 Natural, P06 DEMO-AGG-HPB HPB Bedding Stone, P07 DEMO-AGG-GA Granular A, P08 DEMO-AGG-SAND Concrete Sand, P09 DEMO-MULCH-BLACK Black Cedar Mulch, P10 DEMO-EDGE-ALU Aluminum Edging 8ft.

The procurement gap, by name, is K08: the pick K08 left the pile of P07 Granular A (demo) at Trenton down to fines, twelve tonnes short, and there is no inbound purchase order covering it. The plan test asserts it on its own line: "P07 at Trenton is the K08 gap itself." The buyer's whole story is turning that gap into a placed order, and the worklist is the place it starts. The yard's own note for the row is The highlighted row is the yard's K08 shortage: stock promised to customers that is neither on hand nor on order. A draft does not cover it; only a placed order does.

The seed carries two other gaps alongside K08, on the same demo image: P05 (Step Slab 24x48) sits below its reorder point at Trenton, and P09 (Black Cedar Mulch) sits below its reorder point at Kingston. The worklist reads through the branch fence, so the buyer signs in to the two Trenton gaps, K08 hot on top and the Step Slab gap beside it; the mulch gap sits at Kingston, outside this buyer's reach, and never reaches this desk. The worklist's on-order leg grades only rows the reorder point table holds, and only the products the seeded POs buy (P02, P03, P08, P10) carry no reorder point, so a seeded supply line never closes a gap the buyer is meant to find.

The home, the tiles and the worklist

The home opens with four tiles: Open purchase orders (the count of orders still open: ordered or partial), Drafts waiting (the count of drafts the buyer has not yet placed), Lines below reorder point (the worklist's count, the figure that is hot when it is non-zero, the empty state when it is zero), Incoming (the count of placed orders with a promised date). On this demo image the figures are: open one, drafts zero, lines below two (K08 and the Step Slab gap), incoming one. Every count reads through the branch fence: the seeded Kingston order and the Kingston gap are another buyer's read.

The reorder worklist sits below the tiles. Every managed line whose projected position fell below its reorder point, worst first, inside the buyer's branch reach. The columns are SKU, product, on hand, reserved, in transit, on order, projected, point, short by, suggest, vendor. The K08 row is the highlighted, picked-on-landing row: P07 Granular A at Trenton, the gap the seed plants on purpose, the row the buyer's draft covers first. The other row is P05 Step Slab at Trenton, the seed's other Trenton gap, in the same columns; the buyer's first draft covers K08 only. The seed's third gap (P09 Black Cedar Mulch) sits at Kingston, outside this buyer's reach, and never shows on this worklist.

On a fresh box the worklist is the seeded two, K08 hot on top. The order the buyer drafts covers K08 and only K08; the Step Slab gap stays on the worklist until its own order lands.

Drafting a purchase order

The draft opens with the shortfall row already in it: one line, the product's code, the suggested quantity (the server's answer, not the app's), the unit cost (the branch weighted average the seed plants). The form carries the vendor, the expected arrival, the ship to, the delivery method (Vendor truck or Pickup; nothing is lit until one is chosen, and pressing the lit one again clears it). Save draft, Place order, Cancel draft sit at the bottom, and the line actions (Change, Remove, Add a line) sit on the lines themselves.

A draft's lines are editable while it is a draft. Change opens the line's editor for the quantity and the unit cost, the two figures a vendor's confirmation corrects; the product is not editable (that is a different line: remove this one and add it). Add a line lists the active catalogue, and Remove takes a line off. Once the order is placed the lines freeze: the server refuses the edit in its own words.

Expected arrival takes a date. The field is the browser's own date control, and under it the words read the date out as it is typed (Tuesday 4 March 2031 (in 5 days)). The form checks the date before it is sent, a guard with three plain refusals for an empty, a wrongly shaped and a not real date; the browser's date field makes the guard hard to trip from the screen. The server holds the same line and refuses a bad shape in its own words.

An empty draft is refused in the server's own words, beside the button. Remove the line and the press answers Cannot order an empty purchase order, add at least one line.; the draft sits open until a line is back or the draft is cancelled.

Place order sits the line on the register and under Incoming. The notice names the new PO number (the next branch counter the real mint draws), the register row reads Ordered, the worklist row drops the picked mark, and the tiles move. The draft form is gone, the home is back.

Cancel draft cancels the order it drafted. Press it and the draft panel closes, the worklist row is plain again and the tiles move back. The cancelled order stays in the register, marked Cancelled, and the placement never happened. A draft waiting shows in the register too, marked Draft, until it is placed or cancelled.

The register and the incoming view

The register on the right reads the purchase orders with their states, Ordered, Partial and Received as outline words, never buttons. Receiving sets them as stock lands; the words stay the same. The order is the server's, newest placed first; the value is the order's money, the status is the order's state. The seeded Trenton PO sits at the top of the register; the seeded Kingston PO is another buyer's read.

Incoming is what the yard plans around. The rows list the PO, the vendor, the expected date (as in 3 days, in 5 days, no date, never an invented calendar), how it is coming (Vendor truck, Pickup, or not set yet), and the line count. A missing date reads no date, never a placeholder. The seeded Trenton PO sits on the incoming list with its seeded expected date.

The branch fence is held by the read. A Kingston PO never shows on the Trenton desk; the worklist, the register and the incoming view all read through the fence. The Trenton buyer reads the Trenton rows, the seeded DEMO-TRT-P01; the Kingston rows, the seeded DEMO-KGN-P02, are a different buyer's read. A read that names a branch outside the buyer's reach is refused in the server's own words, You do not have access to that branch.

Receiving, vendors, vendor bills

Receiving is later. The tab on the bar is dashed between the others, and the screen names it in plain text. The yard team's Yard Pick and Receive is the screen that lands the goods; the procurement manager's desk is the screen that drafts and tracks the order, not the screen that closes the gap on the yard's count.

Vendors is later. The tab on the bar is dashed between the others, and the screen names it in plain text. The vendor list, the create, the edit and the deactivate are later items; the desk has the desk's contact, the SKUs and the lead for each vendor, and the rest is later. When the create comes, a vendor made here gets its number from the server, minted under the VM- prefix, never typed, and a seeded supplier keeps its own number.

Vendor bills is later. There is no vendor bill control on this desk; the screen names the words Posting a vendor bill is for Payables and Receivables. The Procurement Manager can read the bills against its own orders and the three way match, but does not post them. The three way match is a Money Desk screen, not a Buying and Stock one.

The walls

Drafting, placing, editing and cancelling a purchase order is for the Procurement Manager, the General Manager and the super admin only. Press the Draft PO button under another sign-in and the words say so, in the gate's shared refusal body, in the app's plain words: Drafting, placing, editing and cancelling a purchase order is for the Procurement Manager, the General Manager and the super admin only. The Yard Manager receives goods only; other roles can read. The bare word Forbidden is not on screen.

The Yard Manager receives goods only. The Yard Manager's sign-in lands the yard's Yard Pick and Receive (later), not this desk; the desk is the procurement manager's, the wall is the role's, the words are the same on both sides.

Known issues you will meet

  • The world is rebuilt to the moment the box was rebuilt, so what you see always reads as current. The next rebuild may carry a fresh seed, and the figures you saw last time may not match the figures you see this time. The shape stays the same.
  • A restart of the box signs everyone out. Sign in again from the role cards.
  • Signing out ends the session. Reloading the page in the same tab keeps the session until you sign out, and a new tab starts at the front door.
  • On a phone the draft fits. The line, its amount and the total are on screen; the page does not scroll sideways. Every worklist figure is on screen, none hidden behind a sideways scroll.
  • With browser developer tools, a technical tester reaches a few reads beyond the desk, and most of them are walled. The Yard Manager's reads are refused for this role, the Yard Manager's sign-in is refused for this role, and the desk refuses its own card under another role in the same words. The wall work for the rest of the routes is a later item, and no General Manager login exists on this box.
