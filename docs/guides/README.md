## The eight roles

The seven role guides, in the order the demo image puts them on the sign-in grid:

1. [Yard Team](./yard-team.md). The yard queue. Claims a pick, pulls the lines, stages the pick. Reaches the branch the front door signs in at, the read level without cost.
2. [Yard Manager](./yard-manager.md). The home bento. Reads the seven widgets, opens the needs row, resolves a shortage. Reaches the branch the front door signs in at, the matrix's own read.
3. [Driver](./driver.md). The run sheet. Marks a stop loaded, en route, delivered (with proof) or failed (with a reason), closes the run. Reaches the one branch the front door signs in at, the moves the matrix names.
4. [Returns, the till's desk](./returns-across-orders.md). The returns surface at `#/returns`. Finds the sale, picks the lines, picks the reason and the refund method, commits. The dealer's approval amount governs a cash return's need for a manager's hand.
5. [Change orders, the till's action](./change-orders.md). The one button on the sold card. Opens the line edit (manager) before the pick, the change order surface (clerk) after it. The server is the authority for which actions the desk offers.
6. [Payables and Receivables](./receivables.md). The Money Desk's receivables side. Works the 18 book invoices and the one bounced payment (DEMO-PAY-B01 on I08). Short, the full brief lives in the part 1 page.
7. [Financial Controller](./controller.md). The Money Desk's controller side. Reads the approvals queue, decides a parked request, approves a parked journal, reads the trial balance. Short, the full brief lives in the part 1 page.
8. [Procurement Manager](./procurement.md). The Buying and Stock board. Reads the open purchase orders, the incoming list and the reorder worklist, drafts, places and tracks a purchase order. The procurement gap, by name, is K08, on P07 Granular A at Trenton. Short, the full brief lives in the part 1 page.
