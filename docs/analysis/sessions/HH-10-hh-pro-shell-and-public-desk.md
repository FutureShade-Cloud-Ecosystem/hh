# HH-10: HH Pro as the standalone public shell, and Public Desk

Goal: ship the `hh-pro` standalone shell (Tauri and PWA, brand-forward) with `pro-procurement`, `pro-pay` and `pro-field`, and the token-credentialed `public-desk`, then move every contractor tenant off the ERP's portal and off the sim-mode HH Pro demo, so the contractor audience has one product.
Preconditions: HH-3 (tenant API, plan and customer-quote modules), HH-4 (packages), HH-7 (quotes the desk produces), HH-8 (stops the field app tracks); D1 (the contractor caches resolved bands labelled as estimates until sync confirms); D2 (homeowner acceptance is a magic-link page under HH Pro's brand with no install); D3 (the ERP portal role set is canonical and HH Pro's roles map onto it once).
Position relative to the ERP cutover session: upstream. The contractor strangler step: the ERP's `portal.html` goes read-only for tenants once they are switched, and HH Pro's sim deployment is retired as a product surface (the sim survives only as the seed generator, HH-5.2). The staff ERP is untouched by this session.
Harness: claude for HH-10.1 (the tenant shell holds the contractor's money and signature paths), HH-10.4 and HH-10.5 (its guards cross the signed acceptance artifact and the brand layer); kimi or kilo for HH-10.2, HH-10.3 and HH-10.6. Integrator and verifier on Claude.
