# HH-6: the HH Field shell and Yard Pick

Goal: ship `hh-field` (the installable phone-first staff shell for yard and driving work, with camera and GPS permissions, identical chrome, an in-shell switcher, device unlock and a per-role landing) and its first micro-app, `yard-pick`, in full offline mode, and move the yard roles onto it while every other role keeps working in HardscapeOS.
Preconditions: HH-3, HH-4, HH-5; D2 in `analysis/DECISIONS.md` (two internal shells split by work context; Yard first); the manifest's `yard-pick` frontend entry (`analysis/role-catalog.yaml`, `analysis/microapps/yard-pick.md`) rehomed from the catalog's `hh-internal` to `hh-field`.
Position relative to the ERP cutover session: upstream. The first strangler step: two yard roles move, the ERP's `yard/yard` unit and the purchasing console's receiving tab go read-only for those roles through the switch built in HH-5.4, and everything else stays in the ERP.
Harness: mixed. HH-6.1 (shell, including device unlock and the deep-link table) on Claude; HH-6.2 and HH-6.3 on Kimi or Kilo with the kits from HH-4; HH-6.4 on Claude. Integrator and verifier on Claude.

Lanes (parallel unless marked):

  Scope: `web/apps/field-shell` and `tauri/hh-field`: identical chrome (org, user, app switcher, sync state, notifications) per context 4.5, built as a shared package the HH Work shell in HH-7 reuses so the two cannot drift; phone-first layout; camera and GPS permissions requested by this shell only (D2: the office shell never asks); the switcher loads only role-allowed micro-apps from the manifest intersected with the org manifest (`yard-pick`, later `drive-run`, and `house-messages` in both shells); the per-role landing carried from `dashboard_role_profiles` and `dashboard_profile_home` (`hardscapeos_dibbits/backend/migrations/0140`, `0141`; catalog open question 8 decides whether it stays a served row or becomes the switcher default); the deep-link table `fb://hh/<entity>/<id>` derived from `hardscapeos_dibbits/app/src/dashboard-nav.ts`'s enumerable registry with the same round-trip property its tests assert; device unlock from HH-4.2 for shared tablets and kiosks (`hardscapeos_dibbits/app/src/kiosk-takeover.ts` is the shipped shared-screen case); the forced-acknowledgement takeover carried as shell chrome.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/field-shell/**`, `hh/tauri/hh-field/**`, `hh/web/packages/shell-chrome/**` (shared with HH-7).
  Acceptance: the mounted-element gate (`hardscapeos_dibbits/app/src/_element-registry.test.mjs`, ported) passes; the deep-link round-trip test passes for every entity; the shell renders offline with the sync state visible; the Tauri shell installs on a tablet and unlocks by PIN.
  Prompt: "Build the `hh-field` shell for web and Tauri per `context/FUTURESHADE-CONTEXT.md` section 4.5 and D2 in `docs/analysis/DECISIONS.md`: phone-first, camera and GPS permissions, identical chrome in a shared package, role-allowed micro-app switcher from the manifest, per-role landing carried from the ERP's dashboard profile tables, the `fb://` deep-link table with the ERP's round-trip property, and device unlock. Port the ERP's mounted-element and layout-contract gates. No em or en dashes; no dates."

- HH-6.2 Yard Pick micro-app
  Scope: `web/apps/yard-pick` on the `scan-and-confirm` kit: the three jobs in `analysis/microapps/yard-pick.md` (take the next pick ticket and pick it line by line with weights and staging; report a shortage rather than pick short; receive a truck against outstanding purchase-order lines), carrying the ERP's yard console API surface (`hardscapeos_dibbits/app/src/yard-console.ts`: `listPickQueue`, `getPick`, `recordPick`, `stagePick`, `submitWeight`, `reportShortage`, `resolveShortage`) and the purchasing console's receiving tab, over the generated client and the offline store in full mode for the scopes the manifest declares, with `manual` review cards for shortages and receipts. Gloves, sunlight, one hand, noise: the doctrine in `hardscapeos_dibbits/.claude/skills/hardscape-ui-design/SKILL.md` and the brief's device notes.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/yard-pick/**`.
  Acceptance: every job completes with the network off and syncs when it returns, proven by a Playwright run that toggles connectivity; the empty, loading, offline, conflict and error states render from the kit; a shortage conflict surfaces a review card and never merges.
  Prompt: "Build the `yard-pick` micro-app from `docs/analysis/microapps/yard-pick.md` on the `scan-and-confirm` kit, over the generated api-client and the offline-store in full mode. Carry the ERP yard console's operations and the receiving tab. Every job must complete offline. No figure of any kind about money appears in this app. No em or en dashes; no dates."
