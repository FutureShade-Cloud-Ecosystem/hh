# HH-9: Money Desk, Manager Home, House Messages, Dealer Admin

Goal: ship the four remaining internal micro-apps in the HH Work shell (with `house-messages` loading in HH Field as well, per D2) and move the finance, leadership, HR and administrative roles onto it, at which point every dealer staff role has a micro-app and the ERP's staff surface is read-only for everyone who has moved.
Preconditions: HH-7 (sales) and HH-8 (logistics), because Money Desk reads the invoices and bills they write and Manager Home decides the approvals they raise; the realtime hub in `core` (from the HH-3 platform template) for House Messages.
Position relative to the ERP cutover session: upstream, and the last internal step before it. After this session the only writers left in the ERP are roles that have not been switched, and the switch is per role.
Harness: claude for HH-9.1 (money) and HH-9.4 (admin and roles); kimi or kilo for HH-9.2 and HH-9.3. Integrator and verifier on Claude.

Lanes (parallel unless marked):
- HH-9.1 Money Desk
  Scope: `web/apps/money-desk` on `list-detail`, online-only: the ledger and posting queue with maker-checker, the period pack (income statement, balance sheet, trial balance, GL detail, AR aging), bank reconciliation (import, match, rules), receivables (aging, credit holds, statements, bounced payments, collections), payables (vendor bill, three-way match), lien holdbacks and tax administration, per `analysis/microapps/money-desk.md`; carried from the ERP's `finance/*`, `compliance/*` and purchasing bills surfaces; period boundaries always through `core`'s house-zone helper, with the house-zone tripwire extended to every new query.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/money-desk/**`, `hh/core/internal/testaudit` (tripwire scope).
  Acceptance: `GATE-5b` (journals balance) and the subledger tie-out pass after a period is closed from the micro-app; the four SQL boundary sites the ERP's own notes list as deferred (`analysis/hardscapeos-inventory.md` section 21.4, items 1 and 2) are either fixed in `core` or listed in the ledger with a reason; a statement run from the app matches one run from the ERP for the same data.
  Prompt: "Build `money-desk` from `docs/analysis/microapps/money-desk.md` on the `list-detail` kit, online-only, carrying the ERP's finance, compliance and payables surfaces. Every period boundary goes through the house-zone helper; extend the tripwire to the new queries. Prove the pack and the tie-out match the ERP's for the same data. No em or en dashes; no dates."

- HH-9.2 Manager Home
  Scope: `web/apps/manager-home` on `dashboard`: the per-role landing leading with one "needs you" widget (the doctrine in `hardscapeos_dibbits/.claude/skills/hardscape-dashboard-widgets/SKILL.md`), the approvals rail with no self-approval, reports and the commission register, the warranty board, per `analysis/microapps/manager-home.md`; the widget catalogue and per-user layouts carried from the ERP's dashboard tables; the ERP's own rule that a shell can never enforce a number it did not compute stays with `core`'s metric providers.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/manager-home/**`.
  Acceptance: an approval raised in `quote-desk` or `dispatch-board` is decided here and the outcome reaches the raising app through the hub; the widget contrast and clipping gates carried from the ERP's home tests pass; read-cache-only offline shows the last measures with their staleness visible.
  Prompt: "Build `manager-home` from `docs/analysis/microapps/manager-home.md` on the `dashboard` kit, carrying the ERP's widget catalogue, role landings, approvals rail, reports and warranty board. Never compute a measure in the client. Show staleness when offline. No em or en dashes; no dates."

- HH-9.3 House Messages on the hub
  Scope: `web/apps/house-messages` on `inbox`: channels, threads, canned acknowledgements, the forced-ack takeover, house queues and notification preferences, per `analysis/microapps/house-messages.md`, carried from the ERP's messaging hub and `messages.html`; the SSE stream replaced by the engine hub (WebSocket plus LISTEN/NOTIFY) in `core`; the SMS arm stays a `worker` job with its quiet window and consent rules unchanged.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/house-messages/**`, `hh/core/internal/messaging` (hub adapter).
  Acceptance: a message posted on one device appears on another through the hub without polling; acks and messages converge under `server_wins`; a deep link from any micro-app opens the record's thread.
  Prompt: "Build `house-messages` from `docs/analysis/microapps/house-messages.md` on the `inbox` kit over the engine hub, carrying the ERP's messaging hub, the forced-ack takeover and the house queues. Replace the server-sent-event stream with the hub in `core`. The SMS arm's consent and quiet-window rules stay as they are. No em or en dashes; no dates."

- HH-9.4 Dealer Admin and the last role switch
  Scope: `web/apps/dealer-admin` on `list-detail`: staff, roles and branch grants; pricing governance (tier rules, category rules, customer overrides, quantity breaks, promotions, price files); audit log; the deployment configuration a contractor sees (branding tokens, terms, company name, feature flags, assistant spend), per `analysis/microapps/dealer-admin.md`, merging the ERP's admin unit and HH Pro's admin console; the credential half (the LLM key, the platform key roster) moves to the operator's launcher section per D4 (`operator-console` is owned by the launcher session, not this path, and is not a manifest frontend); then the runbook and switch for `general_manager`, `financial_controller`, `hr`, `payables_receivables`, `branch_manager`, `super_admin` and `dealer_admin`, with the ERP's `finance`, `compliance`, `reporting`, `messaging`, `warranty` and `admin` units read-only for the moved roles.
  Touches: `hh/web/apps/dealer-admin/**`, `hh/docs/runbooks/role-switch-office.md`, `hh/core/internal/cutover/`.
  Acceptance: a branding change made here is visible on a contractor's next load with no flash of default branding (the specificity rule from `hh_pro_dibbits/CLAUDE.md`, "branding must win on specificity"); no route returns a credential (the write-only test from HH Pro's admin suite ported); the RBAC sweep covers the switched roles; the strangler ledger shows every internal role moved.
  Prompt: "Build `dealer-admin` from `docs/analysis/microapps/dealer-admin.md`, merging the ERP admin unit and HH Pro's admin console; credentials move to the launcher and never have a route out. Then write the runbook and switch for the office roles. After this every internal role is on the shell. No em or en dashes; no dates."

Integrator: 9.3 first (the hub adapter is a `core` change the other apps' notifications ride on); 9.1, 9.2 and 9.4's app together; 9.4's switch last. Expected conflict: the approvals rail component between `manager-home` and `dealer-admin`; one component in the design system, two mounts.
Verifier: `ci` and `fb check` green; `GATE-5b` and the tie-out green from a period closed in the app; the hub end-to-end test green; the credential scan green; RBAC sweep green over every switched role; the readiness report green; the strangler ledger complete for internal roles.

Produces: `money-desk`; `manager-home`; `house-messages`; `dealer-admin`; the hub in `core`; the office role-switch runbook; a complete internal strangler ledger.
Unlocks: HH-11 (the ERP cutover) once HH-10 has moved the contractors.
