# HH-7: the HH Work shell, Counter Till and Quote Desk

Goal: ship `hh-work` (the installable office shell, desktop and tablet, no camera or location permission) with `counter-till` and `quote-desk` as separate micro-apps over a shared quote-to-order entity view, and move inside sales, outside sales and the sales manager onto them, with the till online-only for money and the quote desk in read-cache-plus-outbox mode.
Preconditions: HH-6 (the shared shell-chrome package, yard roles moved); D1 implemented in `core` (HH-3.6 and the pricing module: contract price final, else base then a replacing quantity break, promotions a separate never-stacking level); D2 (two shells, two micro-apps); the tax engine and the margin gate carried in `core`.
Position relative to the ERP cutover session: upstream. Second strangler step: the ERP's `sales/*` unit goes read-only for the moved roles; finance, dispatch, purchasing and admin stay in the ERP.
Harness: claude for HH-7.1 (the till moves money), HH-7.3 and HH-7.5 (provisioning carries the guardrails that keep a public org harmless); kimi or kilo for HH-7.2, HH-7.4, HH-7.6 and HH-7.7. Integrator and verifier on Claude.

- HH-7.5 The `hh-demo` org, provisioned
  Scope: `hh-demo` is provisioned as an ordinary org from `impl-hh-demo` (HH-5.6) by the platform's org-provisioning runbook and by nothing else, which is why it can exist here: the yard moved in HH-6 and the counter and the quote desk move in this session, so the first walkthrough finally has every screen it crosses. The five demo guardrails are configuration of that implementation rather than code in the product: the demo banner is a manifest flag rendered by the shared shell chrome from HH-6.1; rate limits sit at the platform edge and the provider firewall; every outbound integration points at a stub with email and push routed to a sink and payments in test mode; demo sessions are issued per role with an expiry against pre-provisioned demo users and there is no signup route; the org resets both on a schedule and from an operator action in the launcher. Export is switched off for the org and the project brain never reads it, per `context/FUTURESHADE-CONTEXT.md` section 11 on tenant-scoped knowledge.
  Touches: `hh/docs/runbooks/demo-org.md`, `hh/web/packages/design-system/**` (the demo banner in the shared chrome), the `impl-hh-demo` repository (manifest flags, stub endpoints, the session and reset policy).
  Acceptance: provisioning is the runbook and only the runbook, proven by provisioning the org twice from it and getting the same org; the demo org's connection target is its own database and a test proves it can reach neither the `dibbits` database nor `hh-staging`; a prospect's session reaches only the micro-apps the manifest declares for their role and the RBAC sweep covers it; the org appears in the launcher's org list for operators and nowhere else. The six guardrails are acceptance, not guidance, and each carries its own check:
    Guardrail, visible banner: a demo banner is on screen in every shell a demo session can reach, asserted by a rendered check per shell, and a shell that cannot render it refuses to serve this org rather than serving it unmarked.
    Guardrail, rate limits: the platform edge and the provider firewall both refuse a burst against the demo org, proven by a burst that is refused and by a normal session that is not.
    Guardrail, stubbed integrations: every outbound integration resolves to a stub, email and push land in the sink and are observed there, and payments are in test mode; a scan over the implementation's configuration fails on any live endpoint.
    Guardrail, expiring sessions: a demo session is refused once it expires and cannot be renewed or extended from the client, and no signup route exists anywhere in the surface.
    Guardrail, reset: the reset runs both from its schedule and from the operator action in the launcher, and after either run the org matches the seed exactly by per-table checksum, including every tour slice reverted.
    Guardrail, no export and no intake: an export attempt against the demo org is refused, and the intake sweep skips the org, so nothing a prospect does can leave the platform as a bundle or enter the project brain, per `context/FUTURESHADE-CONTEXT.md` section 11 on tenant-scoped knowledge.
  Prompt: "Provision `hh-demo` from the `impl-hh-demo` implementation repository using the platform's org-provisioning runbook, and write `docs/runbooks/demo-org.md`. The guardrails are configuration of the implementation, not code in `hh`: banner flag, edge rate limits, stubbed outbound integrations with a mail and push sink, payments in test mode, expiring per-role sessions with no signup, and a reset that runs both on a schedule and from an operator action. Prove the org is isolated from every other org's database. No em or en dashes; no dates."
